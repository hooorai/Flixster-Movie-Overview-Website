Flixster

The Flixster is a website watch list guide that helps users find movies and series easily. It will be organized by different genres like Horror, Comedy, Documentary, and Thriller with simple recommendations. Each movie or series will have a page with key details such as a summary, cast, and released date. The website will be easy to use on both desktops and mobile devices, with clear navigation to help users quickly find what they're looking for.

Mission

To provide movie lovers with an easy-to-navigate, comprehensive online catalog that makes discovering and exploring films simple. We aim to create a website where users can find detailed information for their preferred movies.

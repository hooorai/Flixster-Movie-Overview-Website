Jojo Rabbit(2019)

PG-13 10/18/2019 Comedy 1h 48m

Overview

In Nazi Germany, a lonely 10-year-old boy, Jojo Betzler, has his world turned upside down when he discovers that his mother is hiding a Jewish girl in their home. A dedicated member of the Hitler Youth, Jojo must confront his deeply ingrained beliefs, aided by his imaginary friend—a goofy version of Adolf Hitler. As he learns the truth about humanity, love, and friendship, Jojo embarks on a heartfelt and humorous journey of self-discovery..

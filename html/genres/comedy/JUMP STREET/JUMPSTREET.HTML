21 Jump Street(2012)

PG-13 03/16/2012 Comedy 1h 49m

Overview

Former high school enemies turned unlikely police partners, Schmidt and Jenko, are sent on an undercover mission to infiltrate a drug ring at a local high school. While Schmidt, who was a nerd in high school, unexpectedly becomes popular, Jenko, once a jock, struggles to fit in. As they navigate teenage social hierarchies, their friendship is put to the test, leading to outrageous situations, unexpected friendships, and a chaotic but action-packed showdown with the criminals.

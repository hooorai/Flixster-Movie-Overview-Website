Game Night(2018)

PG-13 02/23/2028 Comedy 1h 40

Overview

Competitive couple Max and Annie host a weekly game night with their friends. When Max’s charismatic brother, Brooks, arranges a special murder mystery game, it takes a dark turn when real kidnappers abduct him. Believing it’s all part of the game, the group embarks on a wild, comedic adventure filled with mistaken identities, dangerous criminals, and unexpected twists. As the night unfolds, they must work together to survive while keeping up with their love for games.

Jumanji: Welcome to the Jungle (2017)

PG-13 12/20/2017 Comedy 1h 59m

Overview

When four high school students discover an old video game console, they are mysteriously transported into the jungle world of Jumanji, where they become the avatars they chose. Nerdy Spencer transforms into a fearless explorer, football star Fridge shrinks into a small zoologist, shy Martha turns into a fierce warrior, and popular girl Bethany is shocked to become an overweight middle-aged man. With only three lives each, they must work together to complete the game and return to the real world, facing dangerous animals, ruthless villains, and their own insecurities along the way.

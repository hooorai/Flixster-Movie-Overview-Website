Overview

Fourteen years after his first journey to America, Kazakh journalist Borat returns to the United States on a mission to offer his daughter, Tutar, as a gift to high-ranking government officials. As father and daughter navigate a politically charged and pandemic-stricken America, they encounter outrageous situations, exposing societal flaws through Borat’s signature satirical humor.
